Join the club

Pick up a bow. The club brings the rest.

Membership puts you on the shooting line every Tuesday and Thursday with club equipment, a coach nearby and a score on the ranking board.

Join the club

  • Shoot free, every session

    Members never pay range fees. Guests book an hour for RM5 or a day for RM12.

  • All the gear is on the rack

    Bows, arrows, arm guards and finger tabs are provided. Bring closed shoes.

  • A coach on the line

    New archers learn stance, draw and anchor before their first full end.

  • Climb the club ranking

    Log your scores after each session and watch your place on the board move.
